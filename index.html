About

Educator, researcher and science communicator

Dr. Aasheesh Raturi is a faculty member in Physics at Dolphin PG Institute of Biomedical and Natural Sciences, Dehradun. His academic journey spans more than a decade of teaching Physics and Biophysics to undergraduate and postgraduate students, along with research in spectroscopy, wood science and science education.

His work connects laboratory-based research with teaching, outreach and public engagement. He has been associated with academic initiatives, guest lectures, workshops, faculty development programmes and science communication activities across India.

He has also been elected as an Academic Associate of IUCAA, Pune, reflecting his growing engagement with astronomy education and outreach.

Research Interests

Interdisciplinary science for knowledge, society and sustainability

🌲

Wood Science & NIR Spectroscopy

Non-destructive assessment of wood properties using Near-Infrared spectroscopy, chemometrics and multivariate modelling.

🤖

AI for Material Identification

ANN, CNN and machine-learning approaches for automated wood species classification and material authentication.

🔭

Astronomy Outreach

Bringing astronomy to rural and Himalayan schools through telescope sessions, outreach programmes and curiosity-led physics education.

🌿

Environmental Physics

Study of greenhouse gas emissions, wetland ecosystems and carbon dynamics in fragile Himalayan landscapes.
