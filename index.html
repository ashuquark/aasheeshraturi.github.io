About

Research-led teaching and community-focused science communication

Dr. Aasheesh Raturi is a committed educator and researcher with over a decade of experience in teaching Physics and Biophysics to undergraduate and postgraduate students. His academic work emphasizes application-oriented learning, research integration in teaching, and the development of scientific curiosity among learners.

His work connects laboratory-based physics with real-world questions in timber quality assessment, biological materials, environmental monitoring and astronomy education. He has organized workshops, faculty development programmes, outreach schools and science communication activities for students, teachers and rural communities.

Research Areas

Interdisciplinary physics for materials, environment and astronomy education

Spectroscopy & Chemometrics

FT-NIR, UV-Vis and vibrational spectroscopy with PCA, PLS, SIMCA and multivariate modelling for rapid and non-destructive material characterization.

AI & Cognitive Modelling

Deep learning and AI-driven models including CNN, MobileNetV2 and ANN for automated timber species identification and biological material discrimination.

Wood Science & Biophysics

Mechanical properties of wood and biological materials using Universal Testing Machine, ultrasonic vibration, microwave testing and NIR spectroscopy.

Environmental Physics

Greenhouse gas flux monitoring, wetland ecosystem studies and interdisciplinary research connecting physics, ecology and sustainable resource management.

Astronomy Education Research

Innovative frameworks for astronomy education, rural science outreach, student engagement and enrolment motivation in physics and allied sciences.

Science Communication

Public lectures, workshops, Hindi science writing, MOOCs and outreach activities designed to make science accessible and culturally rooted.
